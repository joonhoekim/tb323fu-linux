# Photo shot list

Camera photos (not screenshots) that would help the documentation and the site. Screenshots cannot show the panel
during boot, the bootloader screens or the physical setup; these can.

Before taking them: no device identifiers in the frame (the fastboot screen prints the device's serial — crop or
cover that line; the same goes for any settings page showing hardware IDs or MAC addresses), no Wi-Fi network names, no faces or
home details in the background, and strip location data (EXIF) from the files.

## Booting

1. The tablet booting mainline Linux with the kernel log / boot summary on the panel (portrait, the whole screen readable).
2. The multiboot menu on the panel (`boot-menu`), with two or three roots listed.
3. The boot summary of the initramfs when it stays in the initramfs (volume up held).
4. The panel during the emergency chord: the "volume up+down held: keep holding 10 s" message.

## Desktops

5. GNOME on the tablet held in portrait.
6. GNOME on the tablet in landscape, ideally with the on-screen keyboard or pen in use.
7. The quick settings panel with Open Device Helper's tile (labeled "Tablet") menu open (charge limit, refresh, torch).
8. SteamOS Gaming Mode on the device (landscape), the library or a running game.
9. SteamOS Desktop Mode (KDE) on the device.
10. Another distribution's desktop (Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch or NixOS) on the device, one photo each if possible.

## Hardware

11. The LED ring lit (in a dim room, so the colours show).
12. The tablet on the keyboard / pogo-pin dock, typing.
13. An external monitor connected over USB-C DisplayPort, the desktop shown on both screens.
14. The tablet with USB-C connected to a PC (the USB network / serial way in), with the PC terminal visible next to it.
15. The camera app or a preview from the front and rear cameras, showing the tablet in hand.

## Rooting and recovery

16. The fastboot screen ("FastBoot Mode", `DEVICE STATE - locked`) — **with the serial line covered**.
17. The stock recovery menu.
18. The red "Your device is corrupt" verified-boot screen (only if it shows up anyway; do not provoke it).
19. The tablet in EDL: black screen with the USB cable to the PC, LTBox showing the EDL connection on the PC screen.
20. The KernelSU manager with the "Switch to Linux" module and its Action button.

## Overview

21. The tablet from the front and the back, plain background, for the top of the site.
22. The tablet next to a PC during a build-and-flash cycle (cable, terminal), as a "how development works" picture.
